## Established mathematics that must be credited

1. Jiawang Nie, *First Order Conditions for Semidefinite Representations of
   Convex Sets Defined by Rational or Singular Polynomials*, author preprint
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/0806.4721. Read the introduction, Section 2.1 and
   the lifting definitions in 2.2; read the rational first-order definitions
   (3.7)--(3.9), the adjacent example/counterexample, and Theorem 3.4's
   statement. The whole 25-page proof was not audited. First-order
   nonnegativity certificates and moment liftings are existing methodology.
   The new construction does not claim either as an invention.

2. Jiawang Nie, *Polynomial Matrix Inequality and Semidefinite Representation*,
   author version 2011-03-25, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.0364. Read the
   introduction and Section 4's q-module matrix-concavity definition (4.2),
   Lemma 4.1, Theorem 4.2 with proof, and Corollary 4.3. In particular,
   the stated theorem assumes S is contained in the interior of its domain.
   Our K4 touches D=0. The present proof treats this case directly, instead
   of silently dropping the hypothesis or attributing a new generic theorem.
   This distinction does not establish that boundary treatment is novel.

3. The bounded-density/Markov moment formulas and interval extremizers used
   to identify K4 are classical. The prior module cites Gosse--Runborg and
   Lasserre. This turn reuses that completed boundary proof; it does not
   claim a new truncated-moment theorem or first interval reconstruction.
   The new affine variables are coordinate-polynomial functionals, NOT the
   ordinary time-moment domination hierarchy ruled out in the previous turn.

## What remains a candidate, and what is not established

- Candidate: one explicit joint K4 lift with six auxiliaries and maximum
  PSD block size three, including all boundary cases and without fixing m.
- It is more than the old fixed-m fiber formula: it changes the optimization
  class of arbitrary affine problems over the full K4 to a finite SDP.
- Not established: minimal block size at d=4; existence of lifts for d>=5;
  priority of this specific construction; stable solver performance; improved
  causal communication; acceptance prospects at TAC or any other journal.
- The separate lower theorem d>=5 excludes SOC, not SDP. Together these
  form one representability research line, not two independently publishable
  papers or a completed exact classification.
